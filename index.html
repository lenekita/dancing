Many bodies.
One movement.

A solitary human figure enters a faceless order. Around him, three chromatic factions stop behaving as individuals and become a single choreographed organism.

The glossy black helmets erase identity. Military tailoring imposes discipline. Blue, red and green embroidery introduces difference, only to make the final unity more visible.

Stillness becomes authority. Repetition becomes ritual. What begins as formation turns into collective euphoria: precise, physical and faintly unsettling.
